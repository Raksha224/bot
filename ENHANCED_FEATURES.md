# 🚀 Enhanced Electrician Chatbot Features

## 🧠 **Intelligent Conversation System**

### **1. Advanced Context Analysis**
- **Conversation Stage Detection**: Automatically detects if you're greeting, gathering info, scheduling, or finalizing
- **Intent Classification**: Understands 10+ different intents (repair, installation, emergency, pricing, etc.)
- **Emotion Detection**: Recognizes frustrated, worried, excited, confused, or urgent customers
- **Service Type Identification**: Identifies specific electrical services (outlets, switches, lighting, panels, etc.)
- **Urgency Assessment**: Automatically prioritizes emergency situations

### **2. Personalized Responses**
- **Dynamic Greetings**: Changes based on customer emotion and context
- **Name Recognition**: Remembers and uses customer names throughout conversation
- **Emotional Intelligence**: Responds appropriately to customer emotions
- **Context Memory**: Remembers previous topics and builds on them

### **3. Smart Conversation Flow**
- **Stage-Based Responses**: Different responses for different conversation stages
- **Missing Information Detection**: Knows what info is still needed
- **Follow-up Questions**: Asks relevant questions based on context
- **Natural Progression**: Flows naturally from greeting to booking completion

## 🎯 **Enhanced Response Examples**

### **Frustrated Customer**
**Customer:** "My outlet is broken and it's driving me crazy!"
**Sarah:** "I can hear the frustration in your message, and I'm here to help! I'm sorry to hear you're having electrical issues! That can be really frustrating and sometimes dangerous.

I'd be happy to help you get that sorted out. Can you tell me more about what's happening? For example:
- What exactly isn't working?
- When did the problem start?
- Are there any unusual sounds, smells, or sparks?
- Is it affecting multiple outlets or just one?

This information helps me understand the situation better and schedule the right electrician for your needs."

### **Emergency Situation**
**Customer:** "I have sparks coming from my outlet!"
**Sarah:** "I understand this is an electrical emergency! For your safety, please call our emergency line immediately at (555) 911-ELECTRIC.

Our certified electricians are available 24/7 for urgent situations. Do NOT attempt to fix electrical emergencies yourself - this could be very dangerous.

Can you tell me what's happening so I can make sure we get the right emergency electrician to you quickly?"

### **Excited Customer**
**Customer:** "I'm so excited to upgrade my electrical system!"
**Sarah:** "I love your enthusiasm! Let's make this electrical project amazing! Great! I'd love to help you with that installation. New electrical work is exciting and can really improve your home!

Can you tell me more about what you're looking to have installed? For example:
- What type of installation (outlets, lighting, ceiling fan, etc.)?
- Where in your home?
- Do you have any specific requirements or preferences?
- Is this part of a larger renovation project?

I'll make sure we schedule the right electrician who specializes in that type of work."

### **Worried Customer**
**Customer:** "I'm worried about the electrical safety in my home"
**Sarah:** "I understand your concern, and I want to help you feel safe and secure. Excellent! Electrical inspections are so important for safety and peace of mind.

We offer comprehensive electrical inspections that cover:
- Electrical panel and breaker box assessment
- Outlet and switch safety testing
- GFCI and AFCI protection verification
- Code compliance review
- Safety hazard identification
- Energy efficiency recommendations

What type of inspection are you looking for? Is this for a home purchase, insurance purposes, or general safety maintenance?"
